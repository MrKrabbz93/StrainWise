# StrainWise Unveils the World’s First AI Cannabis Sommelier: A New Era of Personalized Discovery

**THE CLOUD / DECEMBER 2025** — StrainWise, a pioneering force in cannabis technology, today announced the official launch of its flagship application, the world’s first truly personalized AI Cannabis Sommelier. Designed to bridge the gap between complex genetic data and the consumer experience, StrainWise provides a premium, science-backed platform for discovering the perfect cannabis match for any mood, physiology, or wellness goal.

In an industry often dominated by high THC percentages and confusing marketing labels, StrainWise prioritizes the science of **Terpenes** and **Individual Physiology**. The app's core engine, "The Mycelium Network," utilizes advanced artificial intelligence to analyze thousands of data points, offering users a level of insight previously reserved for master cultivators and lab technicians.

### Key Features of the StrainWise Experience:

*   **The AI Sommelier (The Guide):** An interactive assistant that understands intent—from "I need sleep without the grogginess" to "Find me a creative spark for my next writing session."
*   **3D Strain Encyclopedia:** An immersive, high-definition visual library allowing users to explore the genetic makeup and terpene profiles of thousands of strains in stunning detail.
*   **The Mycelium Network:** A first-of-its-kind community engagement system where users earn XP and unlock legendary ranks by contributing high-quality data and reviews.
*   **Live Inventory Intelligence:** A real-time map integration that connects users directly with local dispensaries carrying their specifically recommended strains.

"We didn't just want to build another directory," says the StrainWise Founding Team. "We built a brain. StrainWise is for the connoisseur who wants to understand the 'why' behind their experience. It’s about empowerment through data, delivered with a luxury aesthetic that honors the plant’s sophisticated future."

To celebrate the launch, the first 1,000 users to join the network will be granted a permanent **"Founding Member"** badge, appearing on their global profile.

**StrainWise is now available for download on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.**

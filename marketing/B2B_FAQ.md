# Partner FAQ: Handling Objections 🛡️

Use these answers to respond to dispensary owners' common questions.

---

## 💰 Pricing & Cost

**Q: Is it really free? What's the catch?**
**A:** Yes, claiming your profile and verifying your menu is 100% free. StrainWise monetizes through optional premium analytics and affiliate partnerships with seed banks, not by charging dispensaries for standard listings. We believe accurate data should be free.

**Q: Will you charge me later?**
**A:** The standard "Verified Profile" will always be free. In the future, we may offer "Premium Spotlights" or "CRM Integrations" as paid upgrades, but you are under no obligation to upgrade.

---

## 🔒 Data & Privacy

**Q: How did you get my data? I didn't sign up.**
**A:** Our AI agents aggregate publicly available information from the web (Google Maps, Social Media, Public Directories) to build a comprehensive map of the industry. We do this to help patients find legal access points. Claiming your profile allows you to **control** this data.

**Q: Can I remove my listing?**
**A:** Yes. If you prefer not to be listed, reply to this email with "Remove" and we will honor your request immediately. However, you will lose the SEO traffic currently driving patients to your store.

---

## 🛠️ Technical & Operations

**Q: Do I have to manually update my menu?**
**A:** For now, yes—or you can upload a PDF. We are currently building integrations with major POS systems (Dispense, Duchie, Greenline). Once you claim your profile, you'll be first in line for automated syncing.

**Q: What do I get access to?**
**A:** You get a Partner Dashboard where you can:
1.  Update your opening hours and contact info.
2.  See how many people clicked "Directions" to your store.
3.  Manage your strain inventory list.

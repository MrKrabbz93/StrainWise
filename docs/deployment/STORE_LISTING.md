## 📄 Full Description (SEO Optimized)

**Experience the future of cannabis discovery with StrainWise, your personal AI Cannabis Sommelier.**

Whether you are seeking sleep, focus, pain relief, or a creative spark, StrainWise uses advanced artificial intelligence to navigate the complex world of genetics, terpenes, and effects to find the perfect match for your unique physiology.

### 🧠 AI-POWERED CONSULTATION
Chat with "The Guide", "The Scientist", or "The Connoisseur". Our AI-driven consultant understands your goals—whether medicinal or recreational—and provides data-backed recommendations from our massive strain encyclopedia.

### 🧪 TERPENE-FIRST SCIENCE
Stop guessing based on THC percentages alone. StrainWise breaks down the terpene profiles (Limonene, Myrcene, Pinene, and more) so you know exactly how each strain will make you feel before you spark up.

### 📸 SCAN & IDENTIFY (Beta)
Curious about a strain? Use our visual analysis tool to identify potential genetics and effects based on bud structure and visual cues.

### 🗺️ DISPENSARY FINDER
Find where your recommended strains are currently in stock. Browse local dispensaries and exclusive deals in your area.

### 🏆 THE INNER CIRCLE COMMUNITY
Showcase your journey in our community feed. Earn XP, unlock legendary badges, and climb the ranks from 'Seedling' to 'Head Consultant' by sharing your reviews and insights.
